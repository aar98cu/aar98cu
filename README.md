## About me

I'm a **software engineer with a background in Computer Science and Mechatronics**, focused on **Flutter development, AI-powered applications, and intelligent control systems**.

I design solutions that bridge **mobile applications, backend services, machine learning, and physical hardware**. My experience ranges from enterprise workflows and intelligent document processing to research in neuro-fuzzy systems and industrial automation.

My engineering approach emphasizes **modular architecture, reliability, traceability, and practical deployment**.

## What I work on

- **Mobile & full-stack applications:** Flutter and Dart, state management, backend integrations, authentication, and business workflows.
- **AI & computer vision:** Document detection, classification, structured information extraction, and human-in-the-loop validation.
- **Research:** Interval Type-2 fuzzy systems, adaptive neuro-fuzzy models, and control for autonomous systems.
- **Embedded systems & automation:** ESP32-based controllers, sensors, actuators, motor control, and industrial process automation.

## Selected projects

### 🧠 [IT2-ANFIS OSTRA](https://github.com/aar98cu/IT2-ANFIS-OSTRA) · Open source

An **Interval Type-2 Adaptive Neuro-Fuzzy Inference System** using a **One-Step Type-Reduction Algorithm (OSTRA)**. The repository includes a MATLAB implementation and an initial Python prototype for adaptive fuzzy modeling under uncertainty.

`MATLAB` · `Python` · `Neuro-Fuzzy Systems` · `Type-2 Fuzzy Logic`

### 📄 Intelligent Document Processing · Applied AI

AI-assisted workflows for **document detection, classification, and structured data extraction**, combining computer vision models with multimodal AI. Designed with a focus on **validation, traceability, and human review**.

`Python` · `YOLO` · `PyTorch` · `Gemini API`

### 📱 Enterprise & Operational Mobile Applications · Software engineering

Cross-platform Flutter applications for **inventory management, operator workflows, scheduling, and digital services**. Built around modular architecture, role-based access, and integrations with backend platforms.

`Flutter` · `Dart` · `Riverpod` · `Firebase` · `Supabase` · `PostgreSQL`

`ESP32` · `C/C++` · `Flow Sensors` · `Motor Control` · `Automation`

### 📡 IoT & LoRa Ecosystem · Embedded / Mobile

An ESP32-based IoT architecture connecting remote sensors over **LoRa**, with a Flutter interface and messaging-based notifications.
